QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Before You
Raise a Glass

Everything you need to know before booking your VINORA experience.

Can I reserve a tasting in advance? +

Yes. We recommend reserving your tasting in advance, especially for weekends and private experiences. Walk-ins are welcome when availability allows.

Can I book a private tasting for my group? +

Absolutely. Our private tasting experience is designed for groups of 6 to 20 guests. It is ideal for celebrations, corporate gatherings, and intimate evenings.

Can I change or cancel my reservation? +

Reservations can be changed or cancelled according to our booking policy. Please contact our team as early as possible if your plans change.

Do you offer non-alcoholic options? +

Yes. We offer thoughtfully selected non-alcoholic alternatives for guests who prefer not to drink alcohol.

Can dietary requirements be accommodated? +

Please let us know about any dietary requirements when making your reservation. Our team will do its best to provide suitable pairing options.

How does the VINORA Wine Club work? +

Choose your preferred membership and enjoy a curated selection of wines each month, along with the benefits included in your selected membership tier.
