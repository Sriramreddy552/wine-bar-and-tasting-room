Wine Categories

From bold reds to elegant sparkling wines, discover carefully selected wines from world-renowned vineyards for every occasion and every palate.

Red Wines

Rich and full-bodied wines featuring notes of blackberry, cherry, plum, oak, and spice. Perfect with grilled meats, steaks, roasted lamb, and aged cheeses.

White Wines

Crisp, refreshing wines with citrus, green apple, pear, and tropical fruit flavors. Excellent with seafood, salads, chicken, and creamy pasta dishes.

Rosé Wines

Bright and refreshing with strawberry, raspberry, watermelon, and floral aromas. A perfect companion for summer evenings and light Mediterranean cuisine.

Sparkling Wines

Delicate bubbles with refreshing citrus and orchard fruit flavors. Perfect for celebrations, seafood, brunches, and unforgettable special occasions.

Dessert Wines

Sweet and luxurious wines featuring honey, apricot, caramel, dried fruits, and vanilla notes. A delightful finish to any meal.

Orange Wines

Crafted using traditional skin-contact methods, these wines offer orange peel, tea, herbs, nuts, and earthy flavors with remarkable complexity.
