REGIONAL EXPLORATION

Every Region Has a Story.

From sun-drenched valley slopes to ancient limestone terraces, every bottle reflects its distinct climate, mineral-rich soil composition, historic vineyard traditions, and master winemaking techniques.

California, USA
Bold Cabernet & Rich Oak Chardonnay

Napa Valley

Sun-soaked valley floors framed by volcanic gravel soils produce intense, full-bodied reds with velvety tannins and deep dark berry notes.

  • Climate: Mediterranean warm
  • Soil: Volcanic gravel & loam
  • Grape Variety: Cabernet Sauvignon
  • Technique: French oak aging
Aquitaine, France
Classic Left & Right Bank Blends

Bordeaux

The historic epicenter of winemaking elegance, where Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot achieve timeless harmony and aging potential.

  • Climate: Temperate maritime
  • Soil: Deep gravel & clay-limestone
  • Grape Variety: Cabernet & Merlot
  • Technique: Traditional barrel cuvée
Chianti, Italy
Vibrant Sangiovese & Super Tuscans

Tuscany

Nestled amidst high-altitude cypress hills, Tuscany crafts vibrant Sangiovese bursting with ripe cherry, leather, and terra cotta warmth.

  • Climate: Warm inland continental
  • Soil: Galestro & Alberese slate
  • Grape Variety: Sangiovese, Cabernet
  • Technique: Slavonian oak cask
Côte d'Or, France
Silky Pinot Noir & Mineral Chablis

Burgundy

The birthplace of pure terroir expression, producing delicate Pinot Noir and crystalline Chardonnay with floral nuance and chalky minerality.

  • Climate: Cool continental
  • Soil: Jurassic marl & limestone
  • Grape Variety: Pinot Noir, Chardonnay
  • Technique: Single-climat fermentation
