The Guided Wine Tasting Experience

A seated flight of six pours, walked through glass by glass by our sommelier — from the lightest white on the table to the boldest red in the cellar.

Overview

An evening built around six glasses

A seated flight guided glass by glass, from the lightest pour to the boldest red on the list.

You’ll be seated at our tasting bar or a private table, and your sommelier will bring each pour out one at a time, starting light and building toward the boldest red in that night’s selection.
