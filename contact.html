Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Vinora. If you still have questions, our team is always happy to assist.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are recommended, especially during weekends and special tasting events. Walk-ins are welcome based on availability.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual attire is preferred to maintain the elegant atmosphere of Vinora, although formal wear is not required.

Can I host private events?

Yes. We offer private wine tastings, corporate gatherings, birthdays, anniversaries, and exclusive celebrations.

Are children allowed?

Children are welcome during restaurant dining hours when accompanied by adults. Wine tasting experiences are reserved for guests of legal drinking age.

Do you offer vegetarian pairing menus?

Absolutely. Our chefs have created premium vegetarian pairing menus designed to complement our curated wine collection.

Is outdoor seating available?

Yes. Enjoy our beautiful outdoor terrace with vineyard-inspired surroundings, available seasonally and weather permitting.
